# Surface brief: Between Bells app (index.html)

Scope: the single-page tool (plan, board, controls, pass, trace editor). Visitor mode: **Operate**, with a Persuade-grade first viewport because judges arrive cold.
Audience/job: a student with a mobility need checks whether each passing period is doable, and leaves with exact asks. Judges must grasp this in 15 s.
Proof: the sample school (synthetic, labelled), the real routing engine, cited speed ranges.
Constraints: static, no key, WCAG 2.2 AA, 375 px, reduced motion.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A school day drawn as a transit journey: rooms are stations, each passing period is a coloured line, stairs and elevators are interchanges, the bell is the departure time. It refuses the campus-map default (grey basemap, blue dot, rounded cards).
OWN-WORLD: Enamel white sheet on cool grey ground, ink casings, seven line colours plus alert red reserved for "short", step-free roundel, Overpass heavy station type, Atkinson Hyperlegible UI, square-ended enamel-sign controls, departure-board rows, strip maps, a perforated pass.
STORY: The visitor sees their day's lines on a true-scale plan, presses Play, watches each line race its bell, flips to Crutches, sees two lines re-route through the elevator and go short, and gets a pass listing exact early-release minutes to take to the school.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 62%: the plan sheet at full scale, both floors side by side, day lines drawn, scale bar. Right: mode selector at top, departure board of 6 transitions with ranges and verdicts, primary "Play my day" button under the board. Thin header strip with the wordmark bullet and Trace / Share.
FORM: Transit line diagram (position 5 on my ordered list of 7), seed key 6f18a518 (degraded roll, no challengers).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
