## Pitch, as the sources report it
Drake Semchyshyn (Blue Valley West High School) built Catalyst "to improve students' academic experience within Canvas". He was frustrated by Canvas as a student: "a bright white background when working late at night" and "teachers forgetting to add due dates".

## The wow moment
Unknown (video not watched). From the write-ups, the demo centres on his own Canvas coursework reorganised into a to-do list with custom notification times.

## Scope reality
- Shown: Canvas sync, to-do list with student-entered due dates, theme colours, custom notification times for unsubmitted work, course classification, scheduling.
- A single-developer project that grew out of daily use.
