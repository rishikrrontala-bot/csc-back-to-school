## Pitch, as the source reports it
Lina Saruhan and Zeynep Ozturk (Castilleja School, Folsom High School): Lexia "is designed to support individuals with dyslexia by transforming printed or handwritten text into a more accessible format customized to each user's reading needs."
Inspiration: they saw "family members, classmates, and community members struggle with dyslexia", and noted that school accommodations "can be inconsistent and typically disappear after graduation".
